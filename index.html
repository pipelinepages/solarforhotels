The Real Problem

Your generator isn't just powering the hotel. It's quietly eating your margins.

Hotels need power almost constantly. Lighting, air conditioning, refrigeration, pumps, TVs, Wi-Fi and guest facilities all depend on it. That makes unstable grid power especially expensive.

01

Diesel Costs Keep Rising

Every hour your generator runs adds another expense to your operating costs. That cost moves with the exchange rate, not with your room rate.

02

Guests Expect Reliable Power

Customers don't care why the grid failed. They remember the uncomfortable room, AC cutting out, Wi-Fi dropping, dark hallway or interrupted service. The few seconds between grid failure and generator changeover end up in a bad review.

03

Generators Create More Problems

Fuel logistics, maintenance, noise and fumes create costs beyond the price of diesel itself. Every breakdown means an emergency repair at emergency prices. Noise and fumes work against your listing because rooms near the generator house don't get booked as much.
